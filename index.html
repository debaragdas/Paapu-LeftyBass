The Journey

Hailing from Nagaon, Assam, Papu Leftybass has carved a unique niche in the Indian music industry. Known for his signature left-handed playing style, his journey is a testament to dedication and rhythm.

Moving to Mumbai, he established himself as a versatile session musician, live performer, and music producer. From collaborating with Indie Routes at the NCPA auditorium to producing tracks as a Bass DJ with Ableton Live Looping, his musical footprint spans regional Assamese roots and mainstream Bollywood soundscapes. He is not just a musician; he is an author sharing the profound experiences of an artist's path.

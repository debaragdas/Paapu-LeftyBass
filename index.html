The Journey

Hailing from Nagaon, Assam, Papu Leftybass has carved a unique niche in the Indian music industry. Recognized by his signature left-handed playing style, his career is a testament to raw rhythm and technical dedication.

Currently based in Mumbai, he is a versatile session artist, live performer, and the mind behind the "Bass DJ Project". From stages alongside acclaimed artists to sharing his insights in his book Journey of a Musician (জাৰ্নি অৱ এ মিউজিচিয়ান), his musical footprint continues to expand across genres.
